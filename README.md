# ✈️ Sustainable Aircraft Fleet Assignment Using MILP

## 📌 Overview

This project develops a **Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP)** model for assigning a limited fleet of heterogeneous aircraft to scheduled flights.

The model determines which aircraft type should be assigned to each flight while balancing three competing objectives:

* ✈️ Aircraft operating cost
* 👥 Unserved passenger demand
* 🌱 CO₂ emissions

The optimisation model is implemented in **Python using Gurobi** and solved in **Google Colab**.

The project also evaluates the solution under different passenger-demand scenarios to study how increasing demand affects aircraft allocation, passenger coverage, operating cost and emissions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

An airline operates a network of eight scheduled flights connecting European cities.

The airline has a limited fleet consisting of:

* A320
* B737
* A220

Each aircraft type has a different:

* Passenger capacity
* Operating cost
* CO₂ emission level
* Fleet availability

Passenger demand varies across flights. If the aircraft assigned to a flight cannot accommodate all passengers, the remaining passengers are considered unserved and incur a penalty.

The objective is to determine the optimal aircraft assignment that minimises:

$$
\text{Operating Cost}
+
\text{Unserved Passenger Penalty}
+
\text{CO₂ Cost}
$$

---

## 🧮 Mathematical Model

### Decision Variable

Let

$$
x_{fa} =
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{if aircraft type }a\text{ is assigned to flight }f\\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

and let

$$
u_f \geq 0
$$

represent the number of unserved passengers on flight \(f\).

---

### Objective Function

The model minimises:

$$
\min Z =
\sum_f\sum_a c_a x_{fa}
+
P\sum_f u_f
+
\lambda\sum_f\sum_a e_a x_{fa}
$$

where:

* \(c_a\) = operating cost of aircraft \(a\)
* \(u_f\) = unserved passengers
* \(P\) = penalty for an unserved passenger
* \(e_a\) = CO₂ emissions of aircraft \(a\)
* \(\lambda\) = CO₂ weighting factor

---

### Main Constraints

#### 1. Aircraft assignment

Each flight receives exactly one aircraft:

$$
\sum_a x_{fa}=1
\qquad \forall f
$$

#### 2. Fleet availability

Aircraft utilisation cannot exceed fleet availability:

$$
\sum_f x_{fa}\leq A_a
\qquad \forall a
$$

#### 3. Passenger capacity

Unserved passengers must cover any demand exceeding the selected aircraft's capacity:

$$
u_f\geq
D_f-\sum_a C_a x_{fa}
\qquad \forall f
$$

#### 4. Binary assignment

$$
x_{fa}\in\{0,1\}
$$

## 🔬 Scenario Analysis

Three demand scenarios are considered:

| ---------------- | ----------------: |
| Normal           |              1.00 |
| High Demand      |              1.15 |

## 💡 Possible Extensions

The current model is intentionally small and focuses on the core MILP formulation.

Several extensions could make the model more realistic:

### Aircraft Scheduling

Introduce departure and arrival times and ensure that an aircraft can only operate flights compatible with its schedule.

### Aircraft Rotation

Track individual aircraft and construct feasible sequences of flights.

### Turnaround Constraints

Include minimum turnaround times between consecutive flights.

### Maintenance

Introduce maintenance requirements and aircraft availability over multiple time periods.

### Crew Scheduling

Integrate aircraft assignment with crew availability and scheduling.

### Uncertain Demand

Replace deterministic demand with:

* Stochastic optimisation
* Robust optimisation
* Scenario-based optimisation

### Multi-Objective Optimisation

Instead of using a weighted objective, generate a **Pareto frontier** between:

$$
Operating\ Cost
$$

$$
Passenger\ Service
$$

and

$$
CO₂\ Emissions
$$

### Sustainable Aviation

The model could also be extended to investigate:

* Sustainable Aviation Fuel
* Electric aircraft
* Hybrid aircraft
* Fuel consumption
* Carbon pricing
